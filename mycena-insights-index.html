Credential control ladder

Where does your organisation sit today?

Five rungs from fully exposed to structurally controlled. Most organisations sit at rung 2 or 3 — believing they are protected because they have MFA or a password manager.

Assess your rung →
1
User-created, user-held credentials
Employees create their own passwords. Organisation has no visibility, no control, no revocation capability.
Exposed
2
MFA and SSO deployed
Authentication layer strengthened. The underlying credential is still user-created and user-held. Phishable at the source.
Partial
3
PAM deployed for privileged accounts
Privileged users governed. General workforce — typically 95%+ of employees — still outside governance. Third parties entirely ungoverned.
Partial
4
Organisation generates all credentials
No user creates their own credential. Organisation generates, distributes encrypted, and controls every credential. Users never see or hold it.
Controlled
5
Full credential assurance — internal and third-party
Every credential governed — employees, contractors, vendors, BPO agents, AI systems. Real-time audit trail. Instant revocation. Continuous compliance evidence.
